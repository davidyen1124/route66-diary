Started the morning in **Kingman, AZ** with a shockingly good **Best Western** breakfast (I know). Actual hot food, decent coffee, and waffles that didn’t taste like foam packaging.

Then we did the museum double-feature: the **Arizona Route 66 Museum** and the **Mohave Museum of History and Arts**—followed by a quick peek at the **Bonelli House** for some “people actually lived like this” energy.

After that we rolled out to the **Hackberry General Store**, which is equal parts roadside shrine, gift shop, and nostalgia trap (in a good way).

We wrapped the day at the **Valentine Board House**—a lonely little time capsule that looks like it’s still waiting for 1956 to come back around.

After that, we rolled through **Seligman** for a quick dose of classic Route 66 kitsch and then pointed the car toward **Williams** for the day (where the elevation politely reminds you that Arizona can, in fact, be cold).
