Started the morning in **Kingman** with a shockingly good Best Western breakfast (I know). Actual hot food, decent coffee, and waffles that didn’t taste like foam packaging.

Then we did the museum double-feature: the **Route 66 Museum** and the **Mohave Museum**—followed by a quick peek at the **Bonelli House** for some “people actually lived like this” energy.

After that we rolled out to the **Hackberry General Store**, which is equal parts roadside shrine, gift shop, and nostalgia trap (in a good way).

We made a quick stop at the old **Truxton Canyon Training School** site (Valentine’s weirdly fascinating “how is this still here?” time capsule), then kept rolling.

After that, we rolled through **Seligman** for a quick dose of classic Route 66 kitsch and then pointed the car toward **Williams** for the day (where the elevation politely reminds you that Arizona can, in fact, be cold).
