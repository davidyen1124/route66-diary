Today was supposed to be a "light" day, which naturally turned into a full mission with a lot of walking and very confident snack logic.

We spent most of the day wandering **downtown Santa Fe**, people-watching, ducking in and out of shops, and pretending we were "just browsing" while absolutely power-touring everything within a few blocks.

We stopped at **Loretto Chapel** to see the famous miracle staircase in person. I can confirm: photos do not do it justice, and yes, everyone there has the same expression of "wait... how did they even build this?"

Lunch was a proper New Mexico move at **The Shed**—enchiladas plus green chile stew, which was exactly the kind of meal that makes you rethink every bland lunch choice you've made in your life.

After that we checked out **San Miguel Chapel**, then made our way through the **Canyon Road** art district. Lots of galleries, lots of "we should totally hang this at home" moments, and a shocking amount of self-control.

We wrapped the day by heading back to **Hotel Santa Fe** with perfect timing for happy hour, which is the kind of elite logistical execution this trip deserves.
