Before **Oatman**, we pulled over at the **Welcome to California Sign** for a quick photo—because road trips are 40% driving and 60% proving you were there.

Then we made it into **Oatman**, walked the old wooden sidewalks, and soaked in the full old-west vibe.

The headline event was the donkey situation: donkeys everywhere, confidently occupying the road like local government officials.

By evening we reached **Kingman**, checked in, and had dinner at **Mr D'z Route 66 Diner**. We asked for a to-go box for our salad… and then completely forgot to take it. We even drove back for it, but the waitress had already tossed it—RIP salad, you were too pure for this journey. Sadly, both the burger and the steak were pretty underwhelming, so we called it early and let the donkeys keep running traffic operations without us.
