Today was a short hop from **Flagstaff** to **Winslow**—a nice break from the longer driving days and a chance to pretend we are “taking it easy” while still doing a full itinerary like absolute professionals.

First stop in town was **Standin' on the Corner Park**. Classic Winslow energy. We did the mandatory gawking, photo ops, and “yes, this song is now permanently stuck in my head” moment.

After that, we headed to **La Posada Hotel**—which is genuinely beautiful. Historic, full of character, and one of those places where every hallway makes you slow down and look around. We also spent time in the **Affeldt Mion Museum** there, which made the whole stop feel less like “quick overnight” and more like a proper destination.

Short drive day, high vibes day, zero regrets day.
