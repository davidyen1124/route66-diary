Day 1 of our legendary Route 66 road trip began with the bold, authentic road-warrior move of... flying from **SJC** to **LAX**.

Nothing says “classic highway adventure” like TSA, gate changes, and paying airport prices for water like it’s a collectible item.

After landing in **Los Angeles**, we checked into **Jamaica Bay Inn** and pretended this was all part of a highly strategic launch sequence.

Dinner was **McDonald’s**, because after navigating airport logistics, the Michelin guide can wait and fries cannot.
