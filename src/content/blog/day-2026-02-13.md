Kicked off the Route 66 adventure by heroically... **not driving Route 66 yet**.
Instead, I flew **SJC → LAX** like a highly committed road-tripper who values legroom and mild chaos.

After landing, I rolled into **Jamaica Bay Inn** for Night One, because nothing says "historic highway journey" like checking in near the airport and pretending this was all part of the master plan.

Dinner was **McDonald’s**, because when you’ve had a long day of aviation and logistics, gourmet means fries that arrive in under four minutes.

Tomorrow: actual road miles, fewer gate announcements, and hopefully fewer "boarding group" life lessons.
