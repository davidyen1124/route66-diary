Day 3 was pure High Desert chaos in the best way: lots of stops, zero restraint.

We kicked off in Barstow at the **Mother Road Museum**, then hit the **Harvey House** like responsible travelers who absolutely have a schedule and definitely stick to it.

Next was **Elmer's Bottle Tree Ranch** in Oro Grande, where someone looked at a pile of bottles and said, “Yes, this is art,” and honestly, they were right.

We rolled through **Calico Ghost Town**, stopped at **Bagdad Cafe**, and grabbed the classic photo at the **Roy's Motel** neon sign.

By evening we reached **Needles** for the night. Gas on this stretch was slow and expensive, which was a fun little reminder that suffering builds character.

We closed out with pizza at **River City Pizza** in Needles and called it a win.
