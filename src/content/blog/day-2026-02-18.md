We drove from **Williams** to Flagstaff today and the weather decided to add a little drama: steady snow, gray skies, and the kind of road conditions that make you grip the wheel like it owes you money. I‑40 was slow but manageable—mostly a cautious parade of semis and SUVs—so we took it easy, watched for slick patches on bridges and overpasses, and enjoyed the surprisingly pretty snow-dusted pines once the nerves dialed down.

Rolled into Flagstaff cold, relieved, and immediately in search of heat. And then (because we’re apparently allergic to staying warm) we went over to the **Riordan Mansion** and did a wander through **Heritage Square**—with my hands absolutely freezing the entire time. 10/10 scenic winter vibes, 0/10 finger circulation.

We capped it off by buying two pizzas for “afternoon tea,” which feels extremely correct in this climate: classy in spirit, feral in execution.

And then we ended the day with a great meal inside **Little America**—warm, cozy, and exactly what our frozen souls needed.
