We started the morning by doing the rarest thing on any trip: actually enjoying hotel breakfast.

**Hotel Santa Fe** came through with a legit breakfast spread, good coffee, and zero regrets. Honestly, it set an unreasonably high bar for the rest of the day.

From there we headed to the **Museum of International Folk Art** and wandered through room after room of pieces that somehow made us feel both cultured and underdressed.

Then came the coffee mission in Lupe: the spot is **Iconik Lupe** on Guadalupe. Famous for a reason—strong coffee, cool vibe, and exactly the right amount of "we should probably be heading out" energy.

After that, we made the push to **ABQ (Albuquerque International Sunport)** and got ready to fly back toward PHX and then SFO. Route 66 ground mission complete; airport mode activated.
