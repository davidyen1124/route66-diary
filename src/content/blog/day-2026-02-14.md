Day 2 was a full Route 66 sampler from the coast to the high desert.

Started at **Santa Monica Pier** to grab a photo of the **End of the Trail** sign, then headed to **Pasadena** for two smoothies.

After that, I made a stop at the **first McDonald’s restaurant** for the classic road-trip checkpoint, did a quick peek at the **Victorville museum**, and then tried **Raising Cane’s Chicken Fingers** for the first time.

Wrapped the day in **Barstow** feeling full and happy with the mileage and milestones.
