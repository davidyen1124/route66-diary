Day 2 finally looked like a real Route 66 day instead of an airport side quest.

We started at **Santa Monica Pier** and got the mandatory **Route 66 End of the Trail sign** photo, because if there isn’t photographic evidence, did the trip even happen?

Then it was off to **Pasadena** for smoothies, followed by a stop at the **first McDonald’s restaurant**—a historic site proving that road-trip nutrition has always been a flexible concept.

We made a quick museum stop in **Victorville**, then tried Raising Cane’s Chicken Fingers for the first time in the name of science.

By night we rolled into **Barstow**, tired, overfed, and extremely confident in our ability to turn every stop into a full production.
