Today was the big push from **Winslow** to **Santa Fe**—the kind of drive where your playlist starts strong, then gets weird, then somehow circles back to confidence.

We made a nostalgia detour at the **Wigwam Motel** in Holbrook, because if Route 66 offers you giant teepee rooms and retro roadside chaos, you say yes and ask questions never.

Later, somewhere in “please let this gas gauge be lying” territory, **Maverik (Gallup)** came through like a roadside guardian angel with snacks, fuel, and the exact energy of “you’re not stranded yet.”

Before we officially called it a Santa Fe arrival, we stopped at **Dairy Queen** at the Casa Blanca gas station—my first time ever trying it. The employee did the legendary upside-down ice cream move like it was a magic trick, and honestly, I was way too impressed for a grown adult.

Then we checked into **Hotel Santa Fe**, and it’s seriously cozy. After a long haul day, this place felt like a reward, and we’re staying here for two nights to reset before the next stretch.
